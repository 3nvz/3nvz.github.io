Copernicus Security Research API Validator Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

Copernicus Security Research operates the API Validator as a private, single-user security-research integration. It is used only with accounts, businesses, and assets controlled by the researcher. It is not offered as a service to customers or the public.

Information processed

The integration may process the minimum information needed for a controlled test:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens authorized by the researcher;
  • researcher-owned user, business, ad-account, and asset identifiers;
  • roles and permissions on those controlled test resources; and
  • API request status codes and response fields needed to confirm or disprove an authorization issue.

The integration does not intentionally access third-party accounts or assets, collect Pinterest passwords, or process Pins or Boards.

How information is used

Information is used solely to authenticate controlled API calls, verify authorization boundaries, restore test permissions after a probe, and prepare a responsible vulnerability report when warranted. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or sale.

Storage, retention, and sharing

Tokens remain on the research workstation and are excluded from test output and reports. Tokens are deleted or revoked when testing ends or when they are no longer needed. Sanitized technical evidence may be kept as long as necessary to coordinate a vulnerability report. Information is not sold or shared with third parties; relevant sanitized evidence may be disclosed privately to Pinterest or its authorized vulnerability-disclosure provider.

Security and user control

The integration uses identifier allowlists, least-privilege OAuth scopes, token redaction, and automatic rollback for reversible tests. The account owner can revoke the integration through Pinterest at any time. Requests to review or delete retained research data can be sent to the contact below.

Changes

This policy may be updated if the integration's data practices change. A new effective date will be posted with any update.
